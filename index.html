In high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), what is the primary purpose of using a guard column upstream of the analytical column?

Correct! Rationale: A guard column is a short protective column placed between the injector and analytical column. It traps particulate matter and strongly bound chemical contaminants, significantly prolonging analytical column lifespan.
